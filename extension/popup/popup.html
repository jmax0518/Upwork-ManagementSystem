Upwork Job Summarizer

Configure where structured job data is sent. On Upwork, open a job and click Summary.

Chrome on the same PC: use http://127.0.0.1:3847. ixBrowser / remote profile: use the URL that reaches your dev machine (LAN IP from Next.js, e.g. http://192.168.x.x:3847, or your public URL). After changing this, click Save settings and choose Allow.

Dashboard pipeline

Jobs are posted as JSON with pipelineStatus: "new". Your dashboard can track bidding, interviews, and outcomes by updating status on the same upworkJobId.
